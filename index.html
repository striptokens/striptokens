07 / Information gain

How to verify a token promotion

A careful review is more useful than a louder claim. Use these checks to separate documented participation information from risky promises.

01

How to verify a token promotion

  1. Identify the organiser and a working contact method.
  2. Match the destination to the documented organisation and terms.
  3. Check HTTPS, domain ownership, redirects and download behaviour.
  4. Confirm dates, reward conditions, selection and exclusions.
  5. Stop if the flow asks for a secret or payment detail.
02

What the promotion does and does not require

✓

Should explain: eligibility, dates, reward conditions, selection, contact and terms.

×

Should not require: a password, account cookie, recovery code, API key, payment card or unofficial download.

03

Warning signs of fake generators and unsafe APK files

  • Claims of hacks, exploits, generators or guaranteed tokens.
  • Modified APK files, unofficial apps or extensions with broad permissions.
  • Requests for account credentials, recovery codes, cookies or payment data.
  • Fake scarcity, fake activity, forced surveys or unexplained redirects.

These examples are safety warnings, not participation methods.
